Getting started

From here to your app.

Mesh Talk can install directly from its in-app marketplace. You can also download a ZIP here and install it yourself.

  1. Pick a pack from the collection and download its ZIP.
  2. In Mesh Talk, open Settings → Appearance for themes, an avatar gallery for people or channels, or Manage sticker packs in the sticker picker.
  3. Choose Install ZIP and select the downloaded file.

Artwork rights for the current collection are still under review. Check each pack’s status and the rights review queue before reusing artwork outside its original context. Made your own? Build and check a pack in Studio, then propose it for the marketplace.
