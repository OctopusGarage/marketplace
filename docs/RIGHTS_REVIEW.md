# Artwork rights review queue

The migrated packs retain their historical IDs, version 1.0.0, and original ZIP bytes. Noto Favorites is retired from the current catalog, but its published ZIP remains available at the immutable URL. Google Noto is a new 40-sticker pack. Their catalog status is `pending` because a complete, reviewable source and reuse permission record is not yet present for every image. `cities` includes `assets/avatars/cities/credits.json`; attribution alone may not establish reuse rights. Google Noto artwork is copied from Mesh Talk's bundled Noto set; its original license and attribution need to be attached here before marking it documented. Do not infer that `pending` means approved for reuse in another product.

For each pack, record the creator or source of each image, the applicable license or written permission, transformations, attribution obligations, and any restrictions. Store these with the source artwork and update the catalog status only after review. The migration deliberately publishes all current packs while this queue remains open, as requested by the repository owner.
